Title: What will we do

What will we do when we have no money?
**Oh, true lovers, what will we do then?**
Only hawk around the town for the hungry old crown,
**And we’ll yodel it over again.**

And what will we do if I marry a tinker?
**Oh, true lovers, what will we do then?**
Only sell a tin can and walk on with my man,
**And we’ll yodel it over again.**

And what will we do if I marry a soldier?
**Oh, true lovers, what will we do then?**
Only handle his gun and walk on for the fun,
**And we’ll yodel it over again.**

And what will we do if I have a young daughter?
**Oh, true lovers, what will we do then?**
Only throw her on our back and walk on for the craic,
**And we’ll yodel it over again.**

So what will we do when we have no money?
**Oh, true lovers, what will we do then?**
Only hawk around the town for the hungry old crown,
**And we’ll yodel it over again,
And we’ll yodel it over again,
And we’ll yodel it over again.**
