Title: Men of the Sea

Donald McKay

I've seen all the sights
From the Clyde to the Horn
Where the waves wash the deck
As you pull through the storm

**And so long
You men of the sea
And so long,
And farewell to thee**

And I've been North and South
Where the Whalefish do blow
Where the lines were rock hard
And all covered in snow

**And so long
You men of the sea
And so long,
And farewell to thee**

And I've been to the lands
Where the cannibals roam
Antripodean isles where it's
Blisterin' warm

**And so long
You men of the sea
And so long,
And farewell to thee**

But I've spent all my life
Haulin' on yards
Swingin' in the tops with
The wind blowin' hard

**And so long
You men of the sea
And so long,
And farewell to thee**

So my bairns have grown up
With their father not home
I'll make up for it now
For I ne'er more shall roam

**And so long
You men of the sea
And so long,
And farewell to thee**

It's been along time
Since I set foot on shore
Since the day that I left
My true love at the door

**And so long
You men of the sea
And so long,
And farewell to thee**
